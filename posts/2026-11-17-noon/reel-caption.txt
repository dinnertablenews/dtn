Pilots were missing one number, so they waited.

For about 90 minutes Monday afternoon, United Airlines pilots couldn't get the number that tells them a plane's final weight before takeoff, so they sat at the gate. FlightAware counted 418 delayed United flights. United says there's no sign it was a cyberattack. It came three days after a bad router grounded every flight in the US.

Source: PBS NewsHour
