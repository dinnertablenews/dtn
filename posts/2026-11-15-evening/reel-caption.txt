At age 7: Last month in Jordan, a country across the ocean, three men fired guns at a church where people had come for a funeral, and nine people died. Police caught all three in three days, hiding in an empty house. A court has now given them the biggest punishment there is. It happened a whole day away by plane, and the grown-ups have it.

At 9: On October 23, three men shot at a church funeral in Karak, Jordan. Nine died, 34 were hurt. Police found them three days later in an empty house; they fired and were wounded. A court has sentenced them to hang, the top rung of Jordan's punishment ladder, unused from 2017 until June.

At 16: Should a state kill the men who killed nine at a funeral? Jordan says yes: three sentenced to hang. Second: a week after the shooting, Jordan bombed a Syrian village and said it hit terrorists. Syria said five died, women and children among them. Can a country hit back across a border?
