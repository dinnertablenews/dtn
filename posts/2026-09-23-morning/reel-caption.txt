Utah Republican John Curtis asked the Senate Judiciary Committee to subpoena Donald Trump Jr. and Hunter Biden. ProPublica reported that Russian oligarch Umar Kremlev paid hundreds of thousands of dollars toward Trump Jr.'s wedding party in the Bahamas. Trump Jr. calls it a gift from a friend. At age 6: The president's son got married, and a very rich man from Russia paid for a lot of the party, even the island it was on. A senator named John Curtis says a toaster is a wedding present and an island is something else. He wants to ask the son about it out loud, in front of everyone. Nobody is in trouble. Asking is the senator's job.

At 12: In 2023, Republicans put Hunter Biden under oath about money from other countries. Now a Russian oligarch paid for much of Donald Trump Jr.'s wedding party. Sen. John Curtis, a Republican, says the rule runs both ways: subpoena both sons. A referee who calls fouls on one team is no referee.

At 17: Is a wedding gift still a gift when your father is president? Curtis says a toaster is a gift and an island party from a Putin-connected oligarch is not. Trump Jr. says it was a present from a friend; his father says it will be repaid. If it's paid back, is it settled, or does the offer itself tell you something?

Why this works at age 17: A teenager can hold two true things at once: a present from a friend is normal, and a president's son is never only a friend. Repayment is the live edge, so the script asks whether paying it back settles it. Expect "why drag Hunter Biden back in?" Curtis says both sons get the same oversight, and "trust us" was not enough the first time.
