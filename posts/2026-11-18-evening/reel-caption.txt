House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for Speaker on Wednesday, 251 to 11, behind closed doors. The full House votes on Jan. 3, when Democrats take the majority with at least 257 seats against the 218 needed. He would be the first Black lawmaker to lead the chamber. At age 7: The people who make our country's laws picked a new leader today. His name is Hakeem Jeffries, and 251 of them voted for him, the way a class picks a line leader. He would be the first Black person ever to run that room. The real vote is in January, and the grown-ups have it.

At 9: Democrats won the House in November, so they get to pick its Speaker. On Wednesday they chose Hakeem Jeffries, 251 to 11. The whole House votes on Jan. 3, and his side has at least 257 seats against the 218 needed. Like a team picking its captain: the inside vote decides.

At 15: Does 251 to 11 mean the party agrees? Seven of the no votes were new members from the left who say voters asked for change, and Jeffries promised 'the highest common denominator'. Second: Trump has pledged to veto bills he dislikes. What can a Speaker with 257 seats actually deliver?

Why this works at age 9: At nine a child is working out rules and if they hold, so the script runs a chain: win the House in November, pick the leader in a closed room, carry the open vote in January because 257 beats 218. The captain is a vote they have sat through. Expect 'why vote again in January?' The whole House gets a say, not just the winners.
