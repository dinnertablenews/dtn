At age 6: The president works in a room shaped like an egg, called the Oval Office. The grown-ups have it.

At 10: Trump filled the Oval Office with gold trim. On Thursday he signed an order calling it a national monument, the label used for national parks. But the order lets any president redecorate, and knocking down walls needs Congress. A do-not-touch sign, signed by the kid who's moving out.

At 16: Trump made his own office a national monument two days after his party lost Congress. Can one president bind the next? The order lets any president redecorate; a lawyer called it legally meaningless. Second: that lawyer says Trump is suing to weaken rules on other old buildings. Preservation, or him?
