Russia and Ukraine have been at war for almost five years. The helpers go back and forth like a grown-up walking between two kids who won't talk. The war is far away, and the grown-ups keep trying.

At 10: Russia and Ukraine sat in one room in Abu Dhabi for two days, their first direct talks in nine months. They agreed on a prisoner swap and little else. So the Americans running the talks saw Putin for two hours, then flew to Zelensky: a referee pulling each captain aside.

Why this works at age 10: At ten they are working out chains of cause and effect, so the script hands them one: a shared room for two days produced a prisoner swap and nothing else, so the envoys went to each leader alone.
