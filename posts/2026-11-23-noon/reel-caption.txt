It is getting warmer. The grown-ups are still working on it.

UN climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, ran two days past their deadline and ended Sunday with no main agreement, the first collapse since Copenhagen in 2009. Monday's draft named fossil fuels six times. Sunday's named them zero times, and the EU, Panama and the Marshall Islands refused it. Smaller deals passed. Next try: Ethiopia, 2027.

Source: BBC News

At age 6: Grown-ups from almost every country spent two weeks in one big room in Turkey, making a plan to keep the Earth from getting too hot. They stayed two extra days, like staying after the bell to finish a group project, and still went home without one. The Earth is getting warmer, and the grown-ups are on it. They meet again next year.

At 12: Burning coal, oil and gas heats the planet, so the EU and island nations wanted the deal to say so. Monday's draft said 'fossil fuels' six times. After Russia and Saudi Arabia pushed back, Sunday's said it zero times. A COP deal works like a jury: everyone has to agree, so the EU's no sank it.

At 13: Is a weak deal better than no deal? Palau said yes and would have signed. The EU, Panama and the Marshall Islands said no, and the longest climate talks in UN history ended with nothing. Second: a few oil states blocked a text most countries accepted. Keep a rule that needs everyone?

Why this works at age 6: A six-year-old reasons from what is in front of them and reads your face before your words, so the script keeps the picture small: a room, a bell, a project not finished. Nothing in it is a flood or a fire. Expect 'is the Earth going to be OK?' Say it is getting warmer and the grown-ups are still working on it, and say it calmly.
