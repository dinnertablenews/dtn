At 9: A lab worker in Siberia who studied plague got sick and died. Russia says pneumonia, cause unknown. A local reporter says she broke a test tube. Doctors put 189 people she met in hospital to watch them, a smoke alarm before any fire. Nobody else is sick.

At 17: Why does the US care about one death in Siberia? A plague-lab technician died, Russia says pneumonia, a local outlet says a broken test tube, and 189 contacts are in hospital. Second: Russia deleted its own warnings. Does that make it more likely, or just less known?

Why this works at age 7: A five-year-old takes one vivid feature, and Cantor says a named disease that can reach them is what frightens at this age.
