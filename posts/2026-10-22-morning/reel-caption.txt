At age 7: Stephen is an American teacher who is 74. Russia kept him in prison for more than four years, since before you started school. On Wednesday Russia let him go. An American helper flew there, said thank you, and now Stephen is on his way home to his family. The grown-ups have it.

At 9: Stephen Hubbard, 74, was seized by Russian troops in Ukraine in 2022 and sentenced to nearly seven years. Russia freed him on Wednesday, no trade. The US envoy called Putin a humanitarian; some Republicans objected. A handshake after a foul: allowed, and the crowd judges it.

At 15: Is a man a humanitarian for freeing someone he should never have held? Witkoff said so; some in his own party objected. Days earlier Trump lifted sanctions on Russian diesel.
