At age 6: An officer who works for the government shot a man in Austin, Texas, after his car was pulled over. The man is in the hospital and the doctors have him steady. It happened on one street in one city. The grown-ups have it.

At 11: An ICE officer shot and wounded a man in Austin on Sunday after a traffic stop. ICE works for the national government, so city police weren't part of it. The mayor wants city police on the investigation anyway, like a second referee checking the call: ICE shouldn't judge its own shooting.

At 15: Who should investigate a shooting when the shooter works for the government? An ICE officer shot a man in Austin on Sunday; the mayor wants city police on the case as "third-party eyes." He also hopes the agent stays in the state. Should that be a hope, or a rule?
