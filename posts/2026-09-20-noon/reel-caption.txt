They see the sick people every day, so they go first.

Congo began vaccinating its health workers against Ebola on Saturday in Bunia, the center of the outbreak. Doctors and nurses go first because they are the most exposed. The Ervebo shot worked in past outbreaks of a related strain, and 70,000 doses are approved for Congo. A trial of 20,000 doses will test it on this strain.
