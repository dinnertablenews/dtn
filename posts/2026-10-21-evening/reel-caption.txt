They haven't seen each other since the daughter was 12, because that home wasn't safe for her. Grown-ups on both teams said the ad was wrong, and the grown-ups have it.

At 8: Ramaswamy and Acton want to be governor of Ohio. His ad has her own mother calling her a liar. It leaves out why they haven't spoken since 1978: Acton was taken from that home at 12 because her stepfather was charged with hurting her. Every fight has a line, and both teams said this crossed it.

Why this works at age 6: A six-year-old takes one vivid feature for the whole event, and here it is a mum being unkind on TV, which is frightening on its own.
