Speaks for the president. Trump has nobody doing it.

Trump posted Saturday from his Florida golf club that he has offered Dana White, his friend and the head of UFC, the job of White House press secretary. Nobody at the White House confirmed it. The job has been empty since Katie Zacharia left after five days in October. White posted a photo of himself behind the briefing-room podium.

Source: PBS NewsHour

At age 7: The president wants a new helper whose job is to stand at a podium and answer reporters' questions. He asked Dana White, who runs the cage-fighting league UFC, and he did it in a post for everyone to read. It's like picking the team captain by shouting it across the playground. Nobody is hurt, and the grown-ups have it.

At 9: Republicans lost Congress on Tuesday. By Friday Trump had swapped his chief of staff. On Saturday he posted that he wants Dana White, the UFC boss, as press secretary, with fighters as "assistants." The White House didn't confirm it. Like a coach who loses big and switches players that week.

At 14: Should the president's spokesperson be someone who spent years fighting with reporters? Dana White runs UFC, once put out a video attacking journalists, and Trump asked him in a post, with six fighters as "assistants." Second: the White House wouldn't confirm it. Is an offer nobody confirms real, or a show?

Why this works at age 7: A seven-year-old reasons from what is in front of them and takes one vivid feature for the whole event, so the script hands them a podium and a shout across the playground and leaves the politics out. Expect "did he say yes?" Not yet. He put up a photo of himself at the podium, and he is meeting the president on Monday.
