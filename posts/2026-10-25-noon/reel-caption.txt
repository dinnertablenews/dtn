At 11: Storm Austen on Friday, Storm Boelo on Saturday night: two storms in two days. Wind at 83mph dropped trees on power lines, so 41,000 homes in Ireland woke up with no electricity. At 15: Is this climate change? The BBC's reports don't say, and one wild weekend proves nothing either way. What they do say: a 24-hour amber warning over most of Scotland is unusual, not unheard of. Second: 41,000 homes went dark, most back by tonight. A system failing, or working?

Why this works at age 15: A teenager can hold two true things: their question has no answer in these reports, and the reports still speak.
