Big storms happen anywhere. This one is parked over Florida.

A slow storm sat over Broward County and dropped 15.3 inches of rain in the 24 hours to Wednesday. Hollywood declared an emergency, parts of I-95 and the Turnpike closed under water, and the roof of Temple Sinai fell in. No one was hurt. Schools in Broward and Miami-Dade are closed Thursday. Another 12 inches could fall by Friday.

Source: PBS NewsHour

At age 7: In Florida it rained so hard that cars stopped in the water on the big roads. Fifteen inches fell in one day, about up to your knee. Nobody got hurt, and kids there get Thursday off school while it drains. The grown-ups have it.

At 8: A slow storm parked over Broward County, Florida, and dropped 15.3 inches of rain in a day. Water covered the highway and stalled the cars on it. Drivers who kept going fast pushed waves into people's homes, like someone running through a puddle beside you. Nobody was hurt.

At 16: Why is it pouring in Florida's dry season? A Weather Service meteorologist blames El Niño, a natural cycle steering storms south. Second: the state warned Tuesday night of 'potentially catastrophic' rain, and Wednesday cars still stalled on I-95. Whose call was it to stay home?
