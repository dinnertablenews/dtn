At age 7: A man named Andy Taylor, who played guitar in a famous band, died. He was 65 and had been sick for a long time. He learned guitar by himself as a boy and got paid to play when he was 13, not much older than the big kids at school. His family was all around him at home, the way yours is around this table.

At 12: A fisherman's son answered an ad for a 'live-wire guitarist' and joined Duran Duran in 1980. MTV had just started, and their videos made them huge. At Live Aid in 1985 they had nothing to say to each other, he wrote, so he quit. Like a star leaving a winning team because the locker room went quiet.

At 15: Is a band a job or a friendship? Andy Taylor quit Duran Duran in 1985 because, he wrote, they had nothing to say to each other. Second: in 2022 a letter read at the Rock Hall said he had stage 4 cancer. He made one more record with them. What do you do with time you know is short?

Why this works at age 15: A teenager can hold two true things at once: he walked out on the band, and he spent some of his last years making a record with the same men. The script asks whether a band is a job or a friendship, then what you do with time you know is short. Expect 'did he regret quitting?' In 2008 he said he always wanted to be in the band.
