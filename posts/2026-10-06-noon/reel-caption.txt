Paramount Skydance closed its $110bn takeover of Warner Bros Discovery on Tuesday. The new company, Skydance, owns HBO, CBS, CNN, Nickelodeon, DC Studios and Harry Potter. At age 6: A company called Paramount just bought Warner Bros, the company that makes the Harry Potter movies. Now one company owns Nickelodeon and Harry Potter together, like two toy boxes tipped into one big box. Your shows don't go anywhere. They just have a new owner.

At 12: Paramount paid $110bn for Warner Bros, so one company now owns HBO Max and Paramount+, CNN and CBS, and Harry Potter. About a dozen states sued, saying one owner would raise prices. They settled on a rule: at least 30 films a year. A referee adding a rule mid-game.

At 14: Who decides if a company is too big? States sued to block Paramount's $110bn Warner Bros deal, then settled for promises: 30 films a year and a news board for CNN and CBS. Second: Paramount's owner hosted a dinner for Trump this year. Does a board he appoints protect the news?

Why this works at age 14: A teenager can hold two true things at once: the states got a settlement, and the board meant to protect CNN and CBS is appointed by the company it checks. Seth Stern of the Freedom of the Press Foundation calls it worthless. Expect "so can the owner change the news?" He says not. The board is his to appoint.
