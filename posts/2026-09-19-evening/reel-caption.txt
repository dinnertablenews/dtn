Payback, they say, for Saudi strikes on Yemen.

Saudi Arabia says Yemen's Houthi rebels fired a ballistic missile at Riyadh at dawn Saturday and that it was intercepted. Residents heard an explosion and later saw smoke near the airport. No casualties or damage were reported. It is the first Houthi attack on the capital since their fight opened a new front in the Iran war.
